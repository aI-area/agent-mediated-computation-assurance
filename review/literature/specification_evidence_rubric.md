# Specification evidence rubric

D: specification declaration or related input content is identifiable, but a model-to-specification comparison is not established by the located evidence. C: a comparison, review or consistency procedure is described for named requirements or model attributes; a complete independently measured fidelity result is not established. F: an observed fidelity result or counterexample is tied to a comparison of requested and generated attributes. F can denote a negative result. None of these literature categories assigns S=SUPPORTED. Comparison scope, outcome and independence must be read with each record. The historical specification_check field mixed declaration and checking content and is not an estimate of verified fidelity. All 40 local S1 publications were processed and source passages were assessed, with relevant chapters isolated for proceedings volumes. This assisted reassessment is not an independent human coding exercise or a reliability statistic.
